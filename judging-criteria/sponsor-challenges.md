# Sponsor and track briefs

These are optional directions, not fixed categories. The primary objective is
still an original working product with a convincing user need.

## Healf: close the wellness feedback loop

The brief describes a roughly $2 trillion consumer-wellness market but asks
teams to start with one painful, specific user problem. Healf's stated customer
pattern is:

- tests and consultations happen once, then disappear into separate apps and
  reports;
- blood tests, wearables and phones contain abundant but disconnected data;
- users do not know what to prioritise or which product/routine fits a goal;
- after making a change, users cannot reliably tell whether it worked.

Choose one direction:

1. **Help me understand my data.** Bring scattered tests, wearables and phone
   data into a clear, useful and actionable explanation.
2. **Is this actually working?** Help a user measure what changed after a
   supplement, diet, training or routine change.
3. **Surprise us.** Find a genuine Healf-customer problem and solve it in a new
   way.

A strong entry should demonstrate:

- a specific user with a specific problem;
- multiple sources combined into one useful view;
- one clear decision or next step, not another overwhelming dashboard;
- change measured over time;
- explicit uncertainty, privacy and personal-data handling;
- an experience someone could genuinely use and potentially buy.

Synthetic or personal data is allowed. Do not present the prototype as medical
diagnosis or advice. Protect personal information and make uncertainty visible.
The Healf team and event attendees are available for customer discovery and
testing; the brief explicitly encourages creative use of event facilities and
people.

## Best use of voice: ElevenLabs + Wispr Flow

Voice is the only input available while running. Go beyond dictation: use it to
capture missing context, understand performance, make a decision or interact
with the product while moving.

The organiser brief recommends using the supplied ElevenLabs and/or Wispr Flow
credits. The listed ElevenLabs prize is three months of Scale.

## Commercial traction: Deepline

This challenge rewards actual traction while running. Have something specific
to pitch and real people in a defined ICP to contact. Log calls and emails, the
number and quality of replies, useful feedback and concrete next steps.
Deepline access uses promo code `RUNNINGHACK`.

## Building on the cloud: Cognition

Use Devin to demonstrate what can be built through a cloud agent while the
runner is away from the laptop. This is optional and does not replace the core
product story.

## Texting: Poke

Use Poke from a runner's phone to automate a meaningful product workflow over
text. Avoid bolting texting onto a product when voice or a browser interaction
is more natural.

## Special build-vlog challenge

Record the complete build process, including screen recordings, agent sessions,
runner handovers and team strategy. Organisers invite teams to share it after
the event for a possible special prize.
