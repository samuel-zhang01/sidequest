# Event-day logistics

This is the practical checklist transcribed from the live preparation guide on
2026-08-29. The page is explicitly described as live and may change during
race day.

## Venue and travel

- **Venue:** London Stadium Community Track, E20 2AE.
- **Entrance:** Gate 2.
- Do not take an e-bike or e-scooter: the stadium area does not permit them and
  nearby parking is unavailable.
- Wi-Fi is available only around home base.
- O2 eSIM QR codes are supplied onsite for track connectivity.

## 10:30–11:30 setup

1. Check in with Luma.
2. Join the event WhatsApp group if not already present.
3. Join the home-base Wi-Fi.
4. Scan the onsite O2 eSIM QR code.
5. Install and verify the event applications below.
6. Claim sponsor credits before the run starts.
7. Lock the team of three; ask in WhatsApp for matching help if needed.

check-in whether RoxFit/Traccar automatically enforce agent pausing or only
provide the official tracking evidence.

## Tracking and leaderboard

- Run RoxFit and Traccar together; without the tracking apps, laps do not count.
- Keep the nominated tracking phone charged and allow the required background
  location access.
- Live leaderboard: <https://app-production-36de.up.railway.app/>.
- Track lap counters are described elsewhere by organisers as the backup.

The preparation page abbreviates the 18:00 submission to team name, members,
one-liner and repository or demo link. “How The Hack Works” lists the stricter
superset: team name, members, project name, one-line description, repository,
demo video and agent session history. Prepare the superset.

## What to bring

- Laptop and charger.
- Charged phone; it is both tracker and voice input.
- Portable charger.
- Running gear and a change of clothes.
- Headphones if useful while building.

## Facilities and support

- Lunch, dinner, live commentary and DJ.
- Showers and toilets.
- Lockers requiring one token.
- Healf and Hyperice recovery area.
- Delfa towels.
- First aid at the wellness area.
- Professional video, drone footage and photography.
- For broken technology or sponsor credits, find event-floor staff.
- For other problems, ask a crew member/volunteer or use WhatsApp.

Recovery equipment listed by the guide includes three zero-gravity chairs, two
Normatec Premier units, one short Normatec Premier, a Hypervolt Go 3, two
Contrast shoulder units and a Hypervolt 3.
